VoiceBridge AI Pay — Privacy Notice

Operated by Chumcred Limited
Last updated: 5 November 2026

Restricted prototype notice: VoiceBridge AI Pay is a controlled, educational demonstration, not a live financial service. PayPal Sandbox transactions are simulated and no real money is moved. The restricted Financial Intelligence Assistant and speech features are available only to authorised testers. This notice describes the prototype's current workflows; provider-specific retention and broader deployment arrangements remain under review.

1. Introduction

Chumcred Limited ("Chumcred", "we", "our" or "us") operates VoiceBridge AI Pay, a demonstration platform exploring accessible digital payments, Nigerian-language speech transcription and AI-assisted financial education.

This notice explains the personal and technical information that may be processed when you use the demonstration, why it is processed, which external providers may be involved, and how to contact us about your privacy rights.

2. Information We May Process

Website and technical information

Our hosting and security infrastructure may process IP-related information, browser and device details, request metadata, error logs, security events and temporary session identifiers.

Speech and transcription information

When you voluntarily submit audio through the restricted N-ATLAS Speech Lab, the audio and resulting transcription may be processed by the configured transcription infrastructure and service providers.

N-ATLAS transcription has been tested in Nigerian English, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo. These are restricted prototype tests, not a guarantee of recognition accuracy. Results can vary by language, accent, pronunciation, device and recording conditions. Review and correct a transcript before using it.

Browser voice preview

Experimental browser speech recognition depends on your browser and device. Depending on the browser, audio may be handled by a third-party speech-recognition service. This preview is separate from the N-ATLAS Speech Lab.

Financial education questions and AI responses

The restricted Financial Intelligence Assistant can receive a typed question or a speech transcript that you have reviewed and chosen to submit. When you give the separate AI consent and ask a question, the question, selected response language and relevant recent text conversation history may be sent to OpenAI to generate an educational response. Reviewed financial education notes may also be supplied to guide that response.

A speech transcript is not automatically sent to the Financial Intelligence Assistant. Importing it into the question field is a separate, user-controlled action; reviewing and submitting the question is required.

Generated speech and audio playback

If you choose to prepare an audible answer and give the separate audio-generation consent, the text of the AI-generated answer and selected language may be sent to Langa for text-to-speech processing. Some playback, including browser-based speech features, may also depend on your device, browser and its providers. You do not have to generate audio to read the answer.

PayPal Sandbox information

The demonstration uses simulated PayPal Sandbox transactions. PayPal handles its own login and test-payment interaction. VoiceBridge AI Pay may process test order references, approval status, capture status, transaction amounts and related technical records. No real-money payment is made.

Privacy correspondence

If you email Chumcred about a privacy matter, we may use the contact details and information you provide to investigate and respond.

3. Information You Must Not Submit

Do not enter or speak sensitive personal, financial or identity information, including:

  • Passwords, PINs or one-time passwords.
  • Bank Verification Numbers (BVNs).
  • Bank account numbers or account statements.
  • Full card numbers, CVVs or card credentials.
  • Identity documents or private financial records.

Speech transcription and educational interactions cannot authorise or initiate financial transactions.

4. Why Information Is Processed

  • To deliver requested demonstration features.
  • To convert voluntarily submitted speech into text.
  • To generate requested educational AI explanations.
  • To produce optional spoken versions of AI answers.
  • To operate simulated sandbox checkout.
  • To protect and maintain the platform.
  • To manage authorised demonstration access.
  • To investigate technical errors and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to privacy enquiries.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Processing is subject to applicable lawful grounds under Nigerian data-protection law, including consent where required and other legally appropriate grounds.

5. External Technology Providers

VoiceBridge AI Pay relies on separate providers for hosting, transcription, educational AI responses, optional text-to-speech and sandbox checkout. Depending on the feature used, providers include:

  • Vercel: website hosting and delivery.
  • Railway: cloud application backend.
  • N-ATLAS and RunPod: restricted speech transcription infrastructure.
  • OpenAI: processing consented educational questions and relevant recent conversation text to generate AI answers.
  • Langa: optional generation of spoken audio from AI answer text when the user requests and consents to that feature.
  • PayPal: simulated sandbox checkout.
  • Upstash: selected usage-limit and security controls.

Service providers may process operational information according to their applicable terms and data-handling arrangements.

6. Financial Intelligence Assistant

The Financial Intelligence Assistant is enabled for authorised, passcode-protected demonstrations. It supports educational questions in Nigerian English, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo. It uses OpenAI and a limited set of reviewed educational notes to produce answers.

To use the assistant, you must unlock the separate demonstration access and consent to sending your question and relevant recent text conversation history to OpenAI. If you choose to transfer an N-ATLAS transcript, you should verify and correct it before separately submitting the question. Audio responses are optional and require a separate consent action where Langa is used.

The assistant is not intended to provide personalised financial advice, access accounts, verify balances or initiate payments. AI-generated explanations may be inaccurate.

7. Consent and User Control

The restricted N-ATLAS Speech Lab requires explicit consent before test audio is submitted for transcription. Users can review the resulting text and decide whether to transfer it to the assistant. Transferring alone does not send a question to OpenAI.

The Financial Intelligence Assistant requires separate consent before an educational question and relevant recent conversation text are submitted to OpenAI. Preparing a spoken AI answer through Langa requires another, distinct consent action. The assistant can be used without requesting optional spoken playback.

Spoken instructions and AI-generated explanations cannot approve or capture payments. The PayPal Sandbox checkout requires separate deliberate user actions. You may decline optional speech or AI processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw consent for future processing, subject to applicable law and processing already lawfully completed.

8. Security and Temporary Access

Restricted speech and assistant features use separate demonstration passcodes, access controls, usage limits and temporary session mechanisms. Access requests and relevant technical metadata may be processed to enforce limits and prevent misuse. Do not publish demonstration passcodes or provider credentials.

Hosting and other providers may generate security and diagnostic records. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for periods reasonably necessary for demonstration operations, security, troubleshooting, responding to requests and applicable legal obligations.

Retention and deletion practices can differ between Chumcred and external providers. We do not claim that all submitted audio, transcriptions or technical logs are automatically or immediately deleted.

Specific provider retention periods and deletion arrangements remain subject to operational verification before broader deployment. For retention enquiries, contact Chumcred using the details below.

10. International Processing

Some hosting, speech or AI infrastructure may process information outside Nigeria. Relevant cross-border processing arrangements must comply with applicable legal requirements.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable laws, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to eligible personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Request portability where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant regulator.

Contact Chumcred to exercise your rights. We may request reasonable identity verification. You may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission .

12. Children's Privacy

VoiceBridge AI Pay is intended for authorised demonstration participants and is not designed as a service directed to children. Any testing involving children would require a separate assessment and appropriate safeguards.
